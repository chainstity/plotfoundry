# PlotFoundry
### Generative Art Space for Pen Plotters & Prints

**From algorithms to art.**

PlotFoundry is a creative space dedicated to generative art, procedural graphics, and algorithmic design.

It brings together a growing collection of independent tools and generators that transform algorithms, mathematical patterns, and creative ideas into unique visual compositions.

Designed for artists, makers, designers, and creative enthusiasts, PlotFoundry explores the intersection of code, geometry, illustration, and physical art.

## Explore the Generators

PlotFoundry hosts a collection of creative generators, each with its own visual language, techniques, and possibilities.

| Project | Description |
|---|---|
| **LandGraph** | Generative single-stroke artwork designed for pen plotters. Each pen can have its own drawing layer. |
| **Poster Generator** | Creates unique poster compositions with every generation. |
| **Print Atelier** | Generates illustrations inspired by risograph and linocut printing techniques. |
| **Linocut Studio** | Creates linocut-style travel posters inspired by thousands of real-world locations. |

More generators and creative experiments are continuously being developed.

## Built for Pen Plotters & Prints

A central idea behind PlotFoundry is transforming digital compositions into physical artwork.

Generators are designed to support vector-based workflows and export options, including:

- **SVG Export** – scalable vector graphics.
- **Layered SVG** – separate drawing layers.
- **Color Separation** – individual SVG files for different colors or pens.
- **Pen Plotter Ready** – vector artwork suitable for pen plotting.
- **Print Export** – artwork prepared for digital and physical printing.

## Creative Philosophy

PlotFoundry is built around a few simple ideas:

- **Generative** – algorithms create unique compositions and visual possibilities.
- **Procedural** – rules, parameters, and mathematical structures become creative tools.
- **Experimental** – every generator explores a different artistic direction.
- **Reproducible** – compositions can be generated, customized, and exported.
- **Physical** – digital art can become something tangible through plotting and printing.

Every generation is an opportunity to discover something different.

## Technology

The generators use web technologies and procedural graphics techniques, with an emphasis on vector output.

Technologies and approaches may include:

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript
- SVG
- Canvas
- Mathematical and procedural algorithms
- Generative design techniques

Each generator is an independent creative experiment and may use its own implementation and technologies.

## Who Is It For?

PlotFoundry is intended for:

- Pen plotter enthusiasts and artists.
- Generative and computational artists.
- Graphic designers and illustrators.
- Printmakers and makers.
- Creative coders and developers.
- Anyone interested in algorithmic visual art.

## Project Vision

The long-term vision is to build a growing ecosystem of generative art tools, bringing together different artistic styles, algorithms, and creative workflows in one accessible space.

From a single continuous line to complex geometric compositions, from abstract patterns to illustrated travel posters, PlotFoundry explores the possibilities of art created through algorithms.
